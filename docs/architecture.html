Does the guard pay for itself?
≈ 1 in 9

The guard costs about $0.0003 on every question that passes the floor. Each time it stops an off-topic question, it saves an answer call of about $0.0025. It pays for itself once roughly 11% of the questions reaching it would have been off-topic. From 1 January, when the answer model doubles in price, that drops to about 6%.

  • It is more than a filter: it also rewrites follow-ups into standalone questions, and retrieval needs that anyway.
  • Its real price is time: about 1 s before the first answer token.
  • These are single-request estimates; experiment E7 below measures them properly.

6 · Planned · multilingual pilot

Five languages, one English index

The Act is published in 24 equally authentic languages with the same structure, so Art. 5(1)(f) has the same ID and EUR-Lex anchor in every version. The pilot covers English, Lithuanian, Polish, Finnish and French. Search always runs in English; only the text shown to the answer model, and the answer itself, switch to the user's language.
